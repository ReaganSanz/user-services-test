<p class="page-eyebrow">Overview of NSDF</p>
<!-- Box containing: "What is NSDF?" -->
<section class="section-card">
    <div class="section-banner">
        <img class="section-banner-image" src="/src/assets/intro/hero-space-crop.jpg" alt="Satellite dish connected to a scientific data network"/>
        <h2 class="section-banner-title">What is NSDF?</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="section-card-content">
    <p>
      The National Science Data Fabric (NSDF) connects scientific data, computing, networks, software, facilities, and people through a federated digital backbone designed to make advanced, AI-enabled research more accessible.
    </p>
    </div>
    <section class="feature-grid" aria-label="NSDF capabilities">
        <article class="feature-card">
            <span class="feature-card-icon" aria-hidden="true">1</span>
            <h2>Vision: </h2>
            <p> A globally connected research environment where scientific investigation is not limited by the scale, location, or complexity of data.</p>
        </article>
        <article class="feature-card">
            <span class="feature-card-icon" aria-hidden="true">2</span>
            <h2>Mission</h2>
            <p> Democratize access to large-scale scientific data through production-grade solutions for storage, movement, processing, discovery, and analysis across commodity systems, cloud, and HPC.</p>
        </article>
        <article class="feature-card">
            <span class="feature-card-icon" aria-hidden="true">3</span>
            <h2>Theory of Change</h2>
            <p> Connect communities, reusable services, and shared infrastructure so that scientific facilities and researchers can accomplish together what no institution can achieve alone.</p>
        </article>
    </section>
</section>
